Tell players that your mod uses Gears on your mod's download page and in its README. They learn that
the mod has a settings page in the game's **Mods** menu, and whether they need to install Gears to
use it.

This page covers the Supports Gears badge, where to put it, and how to describe your mod's use of
Gears.

## The Supports Gears badge

![The Supports Gears badge: a rusted gear beside the words SUPPORTS GEARS](/images/Supports-Gears-badge.png)

The badge is a 1000 × 400 PNG with a transparent background. Use it at this URL, or save a copy and upload
it with your mod's other images:

```text
https://s7092910.github.io/Gears/images/Supports-Gears-badge.png
```

When you use the badge:

- Scale it evenly. Keep its 5:2 shape, such as 250 × 100 or 500 × 200.
- Do not crop it, recolor it, or add to it.
- Place it on a dark background. The word "SUPPORTS" is a light silver that is hard to read on white
  or other light colors. Nexus Mods and GitHub's dark theme both work. On a light page, check that
  the whole badge is readable.
- Put it where players look for requirements, such as the top of your description or a requirements
  section.
- Link it to the [Gears page on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/4017), so
  players can install Gears from it.

## Add the badge to your mod's pages

### Nexus Mods

Nexus Mods descriptions use BBCode. Paste this where you want the badge to appear:

```text
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/4017][img]https://s7092910.github.io/Gears/images/Supports-Gears-badge.png[/img][/url]
```

If Gears is required, also add it under **Requirements** on your mod's page, so Nexus Mods lists it
as a dependency.

### GitHub README

Markdown shows the badge at full size. Use HTML to set a smaller width:

```html
<a href="https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/4017">
  <img src="https://s7092910.github.io/Gears/images/Supports-Gears-badge.png" alt="Supports Gears" width="250">
</a>
```

## Describe how your mod uses Gears

Say plainly whether players need Gears. Whether they do depends on how you built your mod. See
[Keep Gears optional](/docs/csharp/getting-started#keep-gears-optional).

| Your mod | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Gears is **required** | "Requires Gears. Install Gears to use this mod and change its settings in the Mods menu." |
| Gears is **optional** | "Supports Gears. Install Gears to change this mod's settings in the Mods menu. Without Gears, the mod uses its default settings." |

Check the optional wording against your mod before you use it. A mod that reads its settings only in
XML patches loses the patches that read a setting when Gears is absent. See
[Read Settings in XML Patches](/docs/xml/xml-patches).

Tell players what they can change, not only that there are settings. For example: "Change the loot
multiplier and the horde night length from the Mods menu."

If your mod has world settings, say so. A world setting is set per world by the host, and players
can't change it while that world is running. Every player in a multiplayer game needs Gears, and so
does the server. For details a player may need, link to
[Gears for Players](/docs/players).

### Language to use and avoid

- Write "Gears" with a capital G. Don't use "GEARS" in prose. The all-capitals form is only for
  the badge.
- Use "supports Gears" or "uses Gears for its settings." Don't write "powered by Gears" or
  "built on Gears." Gears provides the settings page, not your mod's features.
- Don't describe your mod as official, endorsed, or reviewed by Gears.
- Don't promise that Gears adds anything beyond the settings page.

## Add screenshots of your settings page

A screenshot of your settings page shows players what they can change before they download. Capture
your mod's page in the **Mods** menu with the setting descriptions visible. For a mod with world
settings, also capture them on the **Mods World Settings** screen, opened from the New Game screen.

If your mod declares a `<Banner>` in its `ModInfo.xml`, Gears shows it at the top of your settings
page. Use your own mod's banner there, not the Supports Gears badge. See
[Check your ModInfo.xml](/docs/getting-started#check-your-modinfoxml).

## List your mod on the Mod Showcase

The Mod Showcase page on the Gears site lists mods that use Gears. To add yours, see
[Join the Mod Showcase](/docs/mod-showcase).
